
# SkillBridge – Academia–Industry Collaboration Portal

## SIH 2026 | SIH26044

SkillBridge is an AI-powered Academia–Industry Collaboration Portal that connects students, colleges, and industries through skill mapping, internships, and placement opportunities.

The platform analyzes a student's resume, identifies technical skills, detects skill gaps, recommends personalized learning paths, and matches students with relevant internship and job opportunities.

---

## Problem Statement

Students often struggle to understand which skills are required by industries and where their current skill set has gaps.

At the same time, colleges and companies need better ways to identify candidates based on actual skills.

SkillBridge addresses this gap by connecting:

- Students
- Colleges
- Industries

---
